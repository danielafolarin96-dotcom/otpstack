# Acceptable Use Policy

**OtpStack** — Last updated: September 15, 2026

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") explains what you may not do with OtpStack. It's part of our [Terms of Service](./terms-of-service.md). Violating it can result in your account being suspended or frozen, purchases tied to the violation not being refunded, and — where required by law — us cooperating with law enforcement or affected third parties.

## Legitimate use

OtpStack is built for ordinary, legitimate account verification — for example, receiving a one-time SMS code to sign up for or log into an app or service you're genuinely using yourself. That's the use case we support.

## Prohibited use

You may not use OtpStack to:

1. **Commit or facilitate fraud** — including opening accounts on other platforms to run scams, deceive other users of those platforms, commit payment fraud, or falsely represent your identity for financial gain.
2. **Send spam or unsolicited bulk messages**, or set up accounts on other platforms whose primary purpose is spam distribution, bulk promotional messaging, or coordinated inauthentic behavior.
3. **Evade a ban, suspension, or restriction** that a third-party platform has already placed on you, your device, or a prior account — using a fresh number from OtpStack specifically to get back onto a platform that removed you for violating its own rules.
4. **Harass, stalk, impersonate, or threaten** any person, or create accounts on other platforms for the purpose of harassment or impersonation.
5. **Facilitate illegal activity** of any kind, including but not limited to activity related to child exploitation, terrorism, unauthorized access to computer systems, or the sale of illegal goods or services.
6. **Circumvent security or verification systems** in a way not intended by the platform you're verifying with — for example, mass-creating accounts to abuse referral/promo systems, manipulate reviews or ratings, or inflate engagement metrics.
7. **Abuse OtpStack itself** — including creating multiple accounts to evade our rate limits or purchase caps, attempting to reverse-engineer or bypass our pricing or fraud controls, or using automated tools against the Service outside of what we've explicitly permitted.
8. **Resell or redistribute** numbers obtained through OtpStack as your own commercial service without our prior written permission.

## How we enforce this

- We may freeze an account we reasonably suspect is violating this policy, pending review, without prior notice in urgent cases.
- Purchases connected to a violation of this policy are not eligible for a refund, including where the underlying reason a number gets banned by a third-party platform is that it was used for a prohibited purpose above.
- We may report activity to law enforcement or affected third parties where we believe the law requires or reasonably permits it.
- We may update this policy as new abuse patterns emerge; material changes will be reflected in the "last updated" date.

## Reporting abuse

If you believe an OtpStack account is being used in violation of this policy, contact **support@otpstack.com.ng**.
