# Privacy Policy

**OtpStack** — Last updated: September 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data OtpStack collects, why, and what your rights are.

## 1. What we collect

**Account data:** full name, username, email address, and authentication credentials (handled by Supabase Auth — we never see or store your password in plain text).

**Wallet and transaction data:** your wallet balance and the full history of top-ups, purchases, and refunds, including amounts and timestamps. We do not collect or store your card or bank details — Paystack's hosted checkout handles all payment data directly, and we only receive a transaction reference and status.

**Order data:** the phone numbers you rent, the service/country you selected, and any SMS/OTP code received on that number. OTP codes are retained only long enough to display them to you and to support dispute resolution if something goes wrong with an order — not indefinitely.

**Technical data:** IP address and basic device/browser information, used for security, fraud prevention, and rate limiting (e.g. detecting abuse patterns like many accounts from one device).

**Support communications:** anything you send us directly, e.g. via email.

## 2. Why we collect it

- To create and secure your account, and let you log in.
- To operate the wallet — crediting top-ups, debiting purchases, and issuing refunds correctly.
- To fulfill number purchases and show you the OTP code you rented a number for.
- To detect and prevent fraud and abuse, including violations of our Acceptable Use Policy.
- To comply with legal obligations, and to respond to legal requests where required.
- To communicate with you about your account, orders, or changes to our Service or policies.

We do not sell your personal data.

## 3. Who we share it with

We share data with the third parties needed to run the Service, and only for that purpose:

- **Supabase** — our database, authentication, and hosting provider for account and application data.
- **Paystack** — our payment processor, for wallet top-ups. They receive what's needed to process a payment; we don't receive your card/bank details in return.
- **5sim.net** — our upstream number provider. We don't send them your account identity beyond what's operationally required to purchase and manage a number on your behalf.
- **Vercel** — our hosting provider.
- Law enforcement or regulators, where we're legally required to disclose data.

Some of these providers may process data on servers outside Nigeria. Where that happens, we rely on those providers' own safeguards and, where applicable, standard contractual protections for international transfers.

## 4. How long we keep it

- Account and transaction data is kept for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period after (for accounting, fraud-investigation, and legal purposes) once you close it.
- OTP codes are retained only briefly — long enough to display them to you and support any dispute about that specific order — not kept indefinitely.

## 5. Your rights

Depending on applicable law (including Nigeria's Data Protection Act), you may have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, correct inaccurate data, request deletion, object to certain processing, and lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. To exercise any of these, contact us using the details below.

## 6. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in (session cookies, set as httpOnly and secure). We don't currently use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

## 7. Children's privacy

OtpStack is not intended for anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect data from minors; if we learn we have, we'll delete it.

## 8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "last updated" date.

## 9. Contact

Questions about this policy, or to exercise a data-protection right: **support@otpstack.com.ng**.
